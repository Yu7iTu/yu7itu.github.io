Hello, I am Yuqi Tu, a first-year PhD student at Boston University's [Faculty of Computing & Data Sciences (CDS)](https://www.bu.edu/cds-faculty/). I received my bachelor's degree in Mathematics (Honors) and Computer Sciences from UW-Madison, where I had the privilege of being mentored by Prof. [Benedek Valko](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~valko/), Prof. [Ilias Diakonikolas](http://www.iliasdiakonikolas.org), and Prof. [Joshua Cape](https://jcape1.github.io/). Before coming to Madison, I studied for two years at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.
